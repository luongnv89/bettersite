Brand identity

One letter, before and after.

The BetterSite mark is a B built from two website blocks. The top one is the site you have today. The bottom one is the site we rebuild.

The mark

What the B means

  1. 1Outlined top bowl

    Your current website: a frame that still stands, drawn in warm stone grey.

  2. 2The grey line

    Dated content inside it: a single bar, like an old layout waiting for work.

  3. 3Solid terracotta bowl

    The rebuild: wider, filled, in our terracotta. Same foundation, better result.

Both blocks share one straight spine on the left, because we keep what works (your content, your name, your customers) and rebuild the rest. Read top to bottom, the B tells the whole service: before, then after.

The “before”: the outlined bowl

Terracotta is the warm signal that means “better”. Keep it for the rebuilt bowl, buttons and key highlights; let cream and navy do the rest. Terracotta (#C2553A) is for display text 24px and up; use Terracotta dark (#A8432B) for smaller text and buttons.

Typography

Inter Bold, tightened

Rebuilt better.Interface text: system UI, regular 400

The wordmark is set in Inter Bold with slightly tight letter-spacing (−0.6 at 34px), then converted to outlines so it looks identical everywhere. “Better” is set in navy; “Site” takes terracotta, so the name repeats the before-and-after of the mark.

Usage

Keep it recognizable

  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the top bowl.
  • Do not go below 20px for the mark or 96px wide for the full logo.
  • Use the files as they are: do not recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects.
  • On dark backgrounds, photos or color use the white logo; for one-color print use the black one.
