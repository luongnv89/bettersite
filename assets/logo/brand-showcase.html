BetterSite brand identity

A small studio that rebuilds outdated websites with modern UI, performance, SEO, and security — free homepage sample within 48 hours.

Design concept

Before → After, in one mark

The mark is a stylized B split vertically. On the left, three muted, fragmented bars represent the existing site — broken, inconsistent, dated. On the right, a single solid emerald bar represents the rebuild — clean, continuous, fast.

Read at a glance, the eye resolves the fragments into a unified form. That moment of resolution is the product.

  • Use the accent #34D399 sparingly — for the right-hand bar of the mark, the "Site" half of the wordmark, primary CTAs, and links only.
  • Never invert the mark's split: the muted "before" is always on the left, the accent "after" is always on the right.
  • Maintain a clear-space margin equal to the height of one fragment bar around the mark.
  • Minimum mark size: 16px (use favicon.svg below 24px — the full mark loses legibility).
